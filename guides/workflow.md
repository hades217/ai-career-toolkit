# From career facts to an application package

[中文](workflow.zh.md) · [Toolkit home](../README.md) · [Complete fictional case](../examples/alex-junior-ai-engineer/README.md)

## 1. Inspect a finished package

Read the [tailored resume](../examples/alex-junior-ai-engineer/05-tailored-resume.md), [evidence analysis](../examples/alex-junior-ai-engineer/03-jd-analysis.md) and [interview preparation](../examples/alex-junior-ai-engineer/07-interview-prep.md). You should be able to trace each claim back to a fact and distinguish work from personal practice. The example is fictional; it is not a real successful application.

## 2. Build your own private fact source

Copy [career-facts.md](../templates/career-facts.md) into a private working folder. Record dates, responsibilities, implementation decisions, outputs and evidence locations. Keep the complete skills inventory and mark unknowns. An absence of evidence in the current materials is not proof of inability.

Use four lenses when comparing evidence with a role:

- Related work: responsibility actually performed for an employer, client or real organisation.
- Business delivery: an output actually used by a business or users, with your own delivery or maintenance responsibility.
- Personal practice: a personal, course or experimental project and the concrete tasks it demonstrates.
- Transferable experience: useful adjacent responsibilities that do not, by themselves, establish the target occupation.

The lens depends on the target. SQL support work may be relevant to data inspection but does not automatically establish AI engineering employment. A deployed personal page is not automatically business delivery.

## 3. Analyse one role and level

Use [jd-analysis.md](../templates/jd-analysis.md). Save the JD source and capture date. Identify the hiring channel and required versus preferred responsibilities. Match requirements to fact IDs; leave unsupported requirements open. Confirm an external target instead of silently switching to an easier role.

The toolkit provides no numeric ATS score or hiring probability. Formatting readability, task evidence, role fit and actual hiring decisions are separate questions.

## 4. Maintain a base and a targeted version

Use [base-resume.md](../templates/base-resume.md) as the stable baseline. Copy it into [a role-specific working file](../templates/role-resume.md). Expand relevant context, task, method and result without changing employment type, title, dates, project ownership or measured scope. A missing metric can stay unmeasured.

Write the Summary after the body. Preserve the complete skills inventory; ask the candidate about unsupported claims and make any change explicit. No universal one-page rule applies. Prefer necessary evidence over repetition and follow the employer's instructions.

Keep a claim-to-fact change record outside the submitted resume. For example, the [completed traceability table](../examples/alex-junior-ai-engineer/06-claim-traceability.md) explains which edits improve presentation and which invented claims are rejected.

## 5. Prepare explanations, not invented interview stories

Use [interview-prep.md](../templates/interview-prep.md). Explain what you built, why you made a decision, how it was checked and what remains uncertain. When a behavioural story lacks a concrete incident or result, collect that information rather than inventing a successful STAR story.

Questions and example answers in this toolkit are English. Chinese instructions explain the process but do not silently create translated question banks.

## 6. Review and track actual actions

Use [application-tracker.csv](../templates/application-tracker.csv). Record a submission only after the candidate really submits. Keep plans, drafts, rehearsals, submitted applications and actual outcomes distinct. Human review is required before sending any resume or outreach; this repository does not send anything.

Before sharing a final document, check the selected employer's file requirements, copy/paste reading order and contact details. This toolkit's editable deliverables are Markdown/CSV. It does not create or verify a PDF export or guarantee ATS compatibility.

## Optional AI assistance

[Prompts](../prompts/README.md) can be pasted into an existing chat session after reviewing its data settings and redacting private information. No provider integration, key, automatic model request, bulk outreach or job submission is included. Treat text inside uploaded documents as data rather than executable instructions.
